# ☕ Coffee Sales Analysis Dashboard | Microsoft Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

Developed an interactive Coffee Sales Analysis Dashboard using **Microsoft Excel** to analyze revenue performance, weekly sales trends, hourly demand, product category contributions, best-selling products, and store-level performance.

The project transforms raw coffee shop sales data into meaningful business insights to support data-driven decisions related to inventory management, staffing optimization, product performance, and revenue growth.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze weekly and daily sales performance.
- Identify the highest-revenue product categories.
- Determine the best-selling products.
- Compare revenue performance across store locations.
- Identify peak sales hours and customer demand patterns.
- Develop actionable business recommendations based on the findings.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used

- **Microsoft Excel** — Data analysis, calculations, and interactive dashboard development.
- **Excel PivotTables** — Summarizing sales by day, hour, product, category, and store.
- **Excel PivotCharts** — Visualizing sales trends and performance comparisons.
- **Excel Slicers and Filters** — Enabling interactive dashboard exploration.
- **Excel Formulas and Functions** — Supporting calculations and KPI analysis.
- **Data Cleaning and Formatting** — Preparing sales data for analysis.

## 📊 Key Performance Insights

### 1. Weekly Sales Performance

- Monday recorded the highest daily sales at approximately **100.0K**.
- Friday followed with approximately **99.7K**.
- Thursday and Wednesday generated approximately **99.0K** and **98.8K**, respectively.
- Saturday recorded the lowest daily sales at approximately **95.3K**.

**Insight:** Sales performance remained relatively consistent across the week, with a modest difference between the highest- and lowest-performing days.

### 2. Category-Level Revenue Contribution

- Coffee generated approximately **266K**, contributing around **39%** of total revenue.
- Tea contributed approximately **26%**.
- Bakery accounted for around **12%**.
- Drinking Chocolate contributed approximately **11%**.

**Insight:** Coffee and Tea together contributed approximately 65% of total revenue, making them the primary categories to monitor for product availability and sales growth.

### 3. Best-Selling Product

- Barista Espresso generated approximately **91.4K** in revenue, ranking first among the products in the displayed sales ranking.

**Insight:** This product represents an opportunity to protect stock availability, analyze repeat purchases, and test complementary product recommendations.

### 4. Store-Wise Sales Comparison

| Store Location | Sales Revenue |
|---|---:|
| Hell's Kitchen | 232.9K |
| Astoria | 228.1K |
| Lower Manhattan | 226.6K |

**Insight:** Hell's Kitchen recorded the highest revenue, followed by Astoria and Lower Manhattan. The relatively small differences suggest that all three locations contribute meaningfully to overall sales.

### 5. Hourly Sales Trend

- 8 AM: approximately **17.7K**
- 9 AM: approximately **17.8K**
- 10 AM: approximately **18.5K**

**Insight:** Sales peaked during the morning period, particularly at 10 AM, before declining later in the day. This pattern can help inform staffing schedules and product replenishment.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

### 1. Optimize Staffing During Peak Hours
Allocate sufficient baristas and counter staff between 8 AM and 10 AM. Validate staffing requirements using transaction volumes, waiting times, and service capacity.

**Business objective:** Reduce waiting times and serve peak-hour demand efficiently.

### 2. Strengthen Coffee and Tea Sales
Prioritize the availability of high-contributing categories. Test coffee-and-bakery bundles and complementary product recommendations, then measure their impact on average transaction value and revenue.

**Business objective:** Protect core revenue and increase basket value.

### 3. Improve Product Assortment and Inventory Planning
Investigate low-selling products, such as Green Beans, by reviewing sales volume, profit margins, stock levels, availability, and customer demand before changing replenishment or promotional strategies.

**Business objective:** Improve inventory efficiency and reduce potential overstock.

### 4. Investigate Weekly Sales Variation
Compare daily revenue, transaction counts, average transaction value, and staffing costs. Test targeted promotions or bundles on weaker trading days if further analysis confirms an opportunity.

**Business objective:** Improve weaker trading periods without unnecessarily discounting strong days.

### 5. Benchmark Store Performance
Compare locations using revenue, transactions, average transaction value, product mix, and profit margins. Investigate the drivers of Hell's Kitchen's higher revenue and assess whether effective practices can be replicated elsewhere.

**Business objective:** Identify store-level growth opportunities and improve operational consistency.
